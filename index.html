Your phone is the tracker.

The front camera watches your face and Headcam turns that into head tracking for DCS, iRacing, MSFS, the hundreds of games OpenTrack supports, and dozens more through free mods. It even streams to OBS on the side, if you want it.

What it does

Turn, tilt and lean, all six

Your head moves six ways and the game gets all of them. On iPhone that comes from the Face ID camera; on Android from a tracking model we built ourselves. Smoothed on the phone and sent at 120 updates a second.

Streams to OBS, fast

Video and noise-cancelled audio go straight into OBS through QuickFeed, our own plugin. A green screen effect without the green screen, background blur and face lighting come with the optional Pro upgrade.

Is it as fast as a real tracker?

Close. In our own testing both versions came in about 30ms behind a dedicated tracker. On Android, where trackers vary a lot, ours left the competition in the dust.

Won't I be looking away from the screen?

Not for the movements that matter. Headcam maps your head to the camera 1:1 by default, so it is the small moves you make anyway, a lean, a glance, a peek, and the game does the same. Want more? Turn up the sensitivity on any axis, or route it through OpenTrack for curves.

Games that never had it

Free mods on GitHub and Nexus add head tracking to dozens of games, with more on the way. Easy in, easy out. Lopari launches any of them in one click.

Nothing leaves your network

No servers, no accounts, no analytics, no ads. Your face stays on the phone. The only things sent to your PC are six numbers describing where your head is, and your video if you choose to stream.

Getting set up

Three steps, no tools. One more if you stream.

1

Mount the phone

Clip it to the monitor or prop it on the desk, front camera facing you.

2

Connect

Same WiFi as your PC. On iPhone you can plug in over USB with the companion app instead.

3

Play

Start your game and look around. That's it. Games with head tracking built in take it from OpenTrack, a free app on your PC. Lopari can launch any of your games with tracking on, if you would like the shortcut.

4

Stream, if you want it

Install the QuickFeed plugin and your phone shows up in OBS as a camera. Green screen effect, background blur and face lighting come with the optional Pro upgrade.
